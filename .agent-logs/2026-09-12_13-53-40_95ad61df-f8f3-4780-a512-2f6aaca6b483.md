## Suggested changes

**1. Decide what to do about the prefetch storm `loading.tsx` unlocks (this is the real trade-off, not the 404).**
Verified in `node_modules/next/dist/server/app-render/walk-tree-with-flight-router-state.js:77-103` and `:143-151`: today a viewport `&lt;Link&gt;` to a `force-dynamic` route with no `loading.js` gets a prefetch response containing only router state (no render, no head). Once `app/product/[id]/loading.tsx` exists, every product `&lt;Link&gt;` entering the viewport triggers a real render up to the loading boundary *including `rscHead`* (the `Metadata` element → `generateMetadata` → `getProductById` → Neon). The prefetching guide (`01-app/02-guides/prefetching.md:31-37,59-62`) confirms per-URL, no client TTL. Net effect on the live site: /s (PAGE_SIZE=24) and / (2 rails x 12) fire up to ~24 Netlify function invocations + ~24 Neon queries per page view, before anyone clicks. With the user low on Netlify credits (memory) and Neon scale-to-zero, this is not free. Options, cheapest first:
- `prefetch={false}` on the PDP links in `ProductCard.tsx`, `CarouselProductCard.tsx`, `WishlistLineItem.tsx`, `CartLineItem.tsx`, `CheckoutReviewItems.tsx`, `SearchSuggestions.tsx`. Skeleton still appears on click as soon as the first RSC chunk streams (before the DB read), just not "instantly". Strictly better than today, zero prefetch cost.
- Hover/touch prefetch wrapper (docs pattern, `linking-and-navigating.md:282-330`) — instant skeleton on click for a hovered card, prefetch cost bounded to cards the user shows intent on. One small client component. Best UX/cost balance for a judged demo.
- Accept the storm. Only reasonable if credits are no longer a concern.
Pick one and record it in the plan/commit body; the plan currently assumes the instant-prefetched fallback with no mention of what it costs.
